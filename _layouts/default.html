Yun Choi

I am a PhD candidate in Political Methodology at Princeton University, where I am also pursuing the Graduate Certificate in Statistics and Machine Learning. I am also the Data-Driven Social Science (DDSS) Fellow and the Quantitative and Analytical Political Science (QAPS) Fellow.

I have an MA in Quantitative Methods in Social Sciences (Data Science focus) from Columbia University in the City of New York, where I was a Data for Good Scholar.

Prior to Princeton, I worked as a data journalist with some of the largest newsrooms in and outside the U.S., including the ABC-Owned Television Stations and Yonhap Television News (YTN).
